Lamu refinery: site map

Independent; not affiliated with Dangote Group; illustrative, not a design for construction. Illustrative location: no site has been selected or surveyed by this study. The plant outline is our own block plan, placed north of Hindi to show its size and a possible link to the port. Land conditions, ownership and the route have not been established. It is not the plot boundary of any land title, and it is not where the plant will be built.

What you see

  • The plant: each coloured block is one part of the refinery, coloured by plant area. Select a block to see what it is.
  • The fence round the plant: about 2.5 km by 3.2 km.
  • The corridor: a pipe track and a road, about 14 km, from the plant to the existing Port of Lamu.
  • The jetty heads: hypothetical loading platforms beside the port's mapped berths, where smaller ships could load LPG, lubricant oil and coke.
  • The SPMs (single-point moorings): buoys out at sea where large tankers would tie up, joined to the port by seabed lines. Two bring crude oil in and three send petrol, diesel and jet fuel out. Their places are illustrative: the water depth there has not been established.

Key

Key to terms

  • Plant area: a group of units that work together, such as crude, conversion or power.
  • Pipe track: pipes laid side by side on low supports at ground level.
  • Jetty head: the platform at a berth with the loading arms that connect to a ship.
  • SPM: single-point mooring, an anchored buoy far offshore for tankers too deep for the port.
  • Indicative: placed to show size and links, not surveyed or agreed.
