About and credits

The disclaimer in full

Independent; not affiliated with Dangote Group; illustrative, not a design for construction.

This is an independent, illustrative concept study of a large oil refinery near Lamu, Kenya. It is not connected with Dangote Group, with any company planning a refinery, or with any government body.

What this is not

  • It is not a design for construction. Nothing here has been checked by a licensed engineer for building.
  • No site has been selected or surveyed by this study. The plant's place on the map is illustrative: it shows size and links, not a plot of land. The port loading facilities drawn are hypothetical.
  • It is not a safety or environmental study. Flare areas and spacings are illustrative; safe distances have not been established.
  • It is not investment, legal or planning advice.
  • The figures are early estimates for one illustrative operating case. They carry a "how sure" label, on the figure itself or once for the page or table it sits in, and the main ones are given as ranges. The labels say how firm an estimate is; they do not establish design accuracy.
